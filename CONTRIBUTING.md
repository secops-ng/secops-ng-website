# Contributing

Welcome. This is the public face of the **Digital Commons for sovereign
security operations** — a community-driven initiative for European
organisations navigating the regulatory baseline.

## Ethos

- **Sovereignty first.** EU-hosted services and EU-origin libraries are
  preferred. Call out alternatives where they exist.
- **Commons, not vendor.** The site speaks in community voice — no
  marketing verbs, no service-tier language, no "clients" or "customers."
- **Durability over flash.** Content should survive a regulatory cycle.
  Reach for primary sources (ENISA, NIS2 text, national CSIRTs) over
  secondary commentary.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue first for anything larger than a typo. Describe the
   intent in one paragraph.
2. Fork or branch, keep changes scoped, and open a PR against `main`.
3. Every PR is reviewed by the Custodian against the language and
   sovereignty guardrails before merge. See `AGENTS.md` and `SOUL.md`
   at the project root for the standard.
4. Site content is split between code (`LICENSE-CODE`, MIT) and
   editorial content (`LICENSE-CONTENT`, CC BY-SA 4.0). By contributing
   you agree to license your work under the matching license for the
   file you touch.

## What does not belong here

- Internal strategy, roadmaps, or commercial framing.
- Names of specific organisations as prospects, partners, or leads.
- Credentials, internal hostnames, or anything secret-shaped.
- Personal pronouns ("I", "me", "my").

When in doubt, it goes in a private repo, not this one.
