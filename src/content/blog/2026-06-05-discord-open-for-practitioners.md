A short note alongside the field-note cadence, because the message is
short: we're supporting a free public **Discord** for practitioners and
operators building **sovereign security operations** in the open. It
sits next to the public repositories, the field notes, and the
contributor lane — one more surface where the work gets done out loud.

The framing is the same framing the rest of the commons runs on: not a
launch, not a product channel, not a funnel. A room where people
working through the same regulatory baselines — **NIS2**, **DORA**, the
**CRA** — and the same shape of operator problem can compare notes
without re-discovering each other's answers in private.

## What's there to chew on

Concretely, the substrate already in public:

- the framework's **portable content layer** — CACAO v2 playbooks,
  OSCAL and D3FEND control mappings, OCSF data shapes, a KPI/KRI
  catalogue;
- three **reference compile targets** maintained side by side — **n8n**
  for the no-code audience, **Temporal** for durable code, **LangGraph**
  for the agentic frontier — each one of three, none the engine;
- the **regulatory mappings** the field notes have been walking
  through — NIS2 Article 21, DORA core articles, CRA Annex I §1 and §2,
  with a D3FEND crosswalk against each;
- the **forward-public hygiene linter**, the field notes, the
  contributor lane, the [contributor playbook][contributors], and the
  five labelled `good first issue` cards on the framework repo.

The Discord is the conversational layer on top of all of that: the
place to ask why a primitive is shaped a certain way, to compare
EU-hostable stacks, to read off how someone else's compile target
binds the same activity body, or to walk a regulatory mapping out
loud before opening the PR.

## Who it's for

People doing the work. Practitioners writing playbooks, operators
running the runtime, contributors picking up a `good first issue`,
people responsible for a NIS2 or DORA or CRA baseline at an
organisation that wants to do it on a sovereign stack. The voice is
the one the rest of the commons uses — community over commerce,
sovereignty as a public good, framework-agnostic by default.

If that's the kind of room you want to be in, the door is open:

The field-note cadence resumes from here. See you on the other side
